# Ofelia

**Ofelia** (formerly Bonitasoft) is the AI orchestration platform for enterprise operations. It orchestrates people, systems and AI agents on a single auditable path, so that organisations can scale their use of AI without losing control of their processes or their data.

Ofelia has two products: **Ofelia Agentic**, which puts AI agents to work alongside the teams and systems already in place, and **Bonita BPM**, its business process automation platform.

Founded in 2009, Ofelia serves more than 150 organisations across 40 countries.
